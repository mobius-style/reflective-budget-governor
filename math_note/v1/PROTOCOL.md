## Questions and rejection criteria
- P1: Is observed-support normalized entropy >=0.70 saturation exactly unanimity at the implemented windows (RBG 3; RHL 5..8)? Reject if any positive integer histogram in the stated domain violates the equivalence, or real implementation disagrees.
- P2: At what repetition count does an n-gram set become independent of multiplicity? Require nonempty normalized word, explicit n and boundary handling. Reject on an exhaustive small-alphabet counterexample. Do not claim invariance from the first copy.
- P3: Which RBG signals and whole-governor outputs can be identical on indefinitely repeated strings? Explicitly account for raw length classification, short-string fallback, history, and hard cap. Reject any whole-governor assertion if a reachable branch distinguishes the proposed pair.
- P4: Is M-guard termination novel or actually implemented? Compare to T1 and the live guard; do not rebrand known well-founded descent as a discovery.
